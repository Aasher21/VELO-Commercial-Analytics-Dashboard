# VELO Commercial Analytics & Portfolio Dashboard (Pakistan Market)

An independent data-driven commercial analysis bridging technical business analytics with Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) route-to-market (RTM) strategy. This project models price sensitivity, demographic trends, and visual trade marketing optimization for the modern oral nicotine pouch category in Pakistan.

---

## 📊 Core Project Analytics

### 1. Market Demand & Regional Insights
* **High-Velocity Hubs:** Segmented volume distribution across primary urban core clusters (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad).
* **Consumer Shifts:** Mapped demographic trends of young adult consumer segments transitioning toward Reduced-Risk Products (RRPs).

### 2. Price Elasticity under Inflationary Pressure
* Evaluated demand variations across varying product tiers: Mellow/Mini (Rs. 200), Original (Rs. 250), and Intense/Max tiers (Rs. 280 - Rs. 300).
* Modeled revenue outcomes and potential brand-switching risks toward premium imported alternatives like ZYN under varying price stress-tests.

### 3. Flavor Optimization & Promotional Execution
* Tracked velocity of anchor SKUs (Polar Mint, Berry Frost) against localized high-potential variants (Mango Flame, Rich Elaichi).
* Simulated data-backed B2B promotional models and customer lifetime value frameworks within current regulatory compliance parameters.

### 4. Trade Marketing "Share of Shelf" Architecture
* Designed a visual layout recommendation model to optimize physical retail display counters and product visibility on the shop floor.
